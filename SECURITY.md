# Reporting a vulnerability

Report a suspected vulnerability in Proof of Done through [GitHub's private form](https://github.com/ALikesToCode/proof-of-done-docs/security/advisories/new), or write to development@sciencestudent.8shield.net. Do not open a public issue for it.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. Leave out the data of real people. I answer within five business days. If Atlassian marks a request as critical, I answer within 24 hours.

The app runs on Atlassian Forge and has no server of its own. If the fault is in Forge, Jira or Rovo, report it to Atlassian on its page [Report a vulnerability](https://www.atlassian.com/trust/security/report-a-vulnerability).
