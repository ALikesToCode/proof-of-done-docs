1. Done contract People write the criteria, or the panel reads them from the description.
  2. Evidence A person or a coding agent submits a piece of evidence for one criterion.
  3. Checks The app checks its fields when it arrives. A check can only turn evidence away.
  4. Verdict A person or the Proof Verifier accepts or rejects it, by default not its author.
  5. Done gate A close without proof gets a comment or a move back. The validator can refuse it.
  6. Proven Every criterion has accepted evidence.
